# Synthetic demo data

Seed: 20261112. Eight SKUs, 700 daily observations each (5,600 rows), generated by make_demo.py.
Contains weekly/annual patterns, different trends, a late shift, occasional bursts, intermittent sales and an all-zero SKU. No real shop, customer, stockout or purchase outcome.
Inventory is a hand-authored end-of-last-day snapshot. Dates are relative to this historical scenario.
The creator knows the generator; chronological splits do not make this independent external validation.
